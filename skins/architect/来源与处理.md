# “建筑师”

依据用户提供的游戏造型截图：e1a91a4aa3d7b479879b7f57e2ac8c7a.jpg

大头像仅作细节参考：4afa33b265bedf1b3eddcba999ea9c30.jpg

内置 imagegen 生成五块透明素材；脸部共用原有无刘海皮肤的同一张贴图、同一剪裁轮廓、同一坐标；没有生成新的脸。原始图集保持不变，坐标由 alpha 连通区域测量取得。盲杖随皮肤绑定。游戏角色造型的小尺寸卡通改编，不是游戏官方贴图。

提示词：

Create a SINGLE layered sprite atlas for a chibi floating-head Helena desktop pet. Image 1 is the EXISTING pet STYLE and PROPORTION reference: preserve its soft rounded warm outlines and gentle pastel 2.5D shaded game art. Other images are COSTUME references only: extract their hair, headwear, glasses and cane designs, never copy their face or bodies. STRICT 3 columns x 2 rows equal-cell atlas, landscape 1536x1024. Reading order: TOP LEFT back hair silhouette with a TRANSPARENT central face opening (no skin, ears, eyes or head); TOP MIDDLE headwear only, fully detached; TOP RIGHT front-facing symmetric round glasses only, two equal lenses and bridge, no face; BOTTOM LEFT a separate short front hair/fringe piece to sit along the upper forehead and small temple locks, no skin; BOTTOM MIDDLE the complete matching slender cane/staff upright, tip and shaft fully visible, small enough to fit the cell; BOTTOM RIGHT completely empty. Each separate asset has generous transparent padding and stays strictly inside its cell, no overlap. Hair frames the SAME existing round face and does not introduce a new face shape. Headwear shape can be distinctive but must fit a big round head. The glasses should retain roughly circular lens centers at 24% and 76% of their total width so existing animated eyes can be reused. Cane must match costume reference; do not substitute a generic cane. True alpha background. No character composite, no face, no neck, no body, no hands, no clothes, no UI, no text, no numbers, no labels, no borders, no scene, no cast shadows outside sprites. COSTUME SPECIFICATION: pale blonde curled bob; pale mint and white low bonnet/cap with broad lacy edge, blue faceted gemstones, pearl beads and silver leaf ornaments; silver round spectacles with mint and blue four-petal flowers in lenses; silver slender cane topped with a hollow geometric polyhedron architectural cage and small mint cross

运行时额外约束：不沿用原皮猫耳或环绕挂件，不生成脸和身体。
