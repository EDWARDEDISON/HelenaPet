# 素材制作记录

使用 Codex 内置 imagegen 工具；没有使用 CLI、外部图像 API 或用户 API Key。输出以真正透明的 PNG 保留在 `skins/original/atlas.png`，程序按配置读取图集，不另做抠图。

第一轮输入：用户给的官方 Q 版头像、游戏正面截图。提示词如下：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D cutout puppet parts atlas for a Windows desktop pet. References: image 1 is the official chibi art style and identity reference for Helena Adams / The Mind's Eye from Identity V, image 2 is costume detail reference. Make ONE square transparent PNG, a precise 2 by 2 grid of FOUR SEPARATE isolated components, no visible grid, no text. Every component fits entirely inside its own quadrant with generous transparent padding, never crossing midpoint. All parts perfectly front facing, symmetrical, same hand-drawn 2D chibi style, soft cream and dusty blue, clean warm dark outlines, watercolor/cel shading, rounded adorable head-only proportions. TOP LEFT quadrant: just the back hair mass: short wavy dusty mauve bob forming a rounded silhouette. No face, no hat, no neck. TOP RIGHT quadrant: just a round pale peach chibi face with small ears and a little mauve fringe at the top and side curl locks. NO EYES, NO GLASSES, NO NOSE, NO MOUTH (smooth blank face is necessary for animation compositing). Face widest at cheek and cute rounded chin. BOTTOM LEFT quadrant: just her dusty blue cloche/bucket hat, silver-white narrow ribbon around crown, cream seashell on viewer right with dark blue fabric rosette, a few ivory repair stitches on viewer left; wide gently curved brim. No face or hair attached. BOTTOM RIGHT quadrant: just her two large round dark charcoal spectacles connected by a bridge, rose-mauve button lenses with white X cross stitches and gentle ivory reflections. No face or hair or hat. These four parts will be cropped and reassembled as one cohesive chibi floating head; do not draw any whole character. True transparent background around and within components, no checkered background drawn, no shadows outside the parts, no scenery, no body, no shoulders, no neck, no hands, no lettering or watermark. Preserve her distinctive hat, bob and button spectacles; do not substitute large human eyes.

第二轮输入：第一轮图集、官方 Q 版头像、游戏正面截图。依据用户“帽子层次有问题，头发可以再厚一点”的反馈编辑，提示词如下：

> Edit target: image 1 is a transparent production 2x2 puppet atlas. Images 2 and 3 are official chibi style and game costume supporting references. Correct ONLY hat construction and hair volume while preserving the cute 2D art style, character palette, four separate parts in the SAME quadrants, and button spectacles. Keep true transparent background and NO labels/grid. TOP LEFT: make the back hair a much fuller, dense, rounded mauve bob with large smooth curled side locks, close to the thick hair masses of reference 2, fewer skinny dangling strands. TOP RIGHT: keep blank pale round face, NO eyes NO glasses NO mouth NO nose, but thicken the two side curled hair masses substantially, cover most of the ears with broad soft curls. Avoid the current tiny ringlet-and-exposed-ear look. BOTTOM LEFT: redraw her dusty blue cloche hat with physically clear SINGLE brim construction. Current hat is wrong because a second dark wide inner brim under a raised visor creates the look of stacked hats or a helmet. Replace this with a continuous simple downward-flaring bucket/cloche brim like references 2 and 3: one crown, one white narrow ribbon, ONE single sloping blue brim, one thin outlined edge. NO broad black/dark-blue mouth-like opening underneath the brim. NO separate raised visor, no extra brim, no doubled edges. The front lower edge should be one shallow smooth downward arc, side edges slightly lower; minimize visible underside to a very narrow edge only. Softer flatter cel shading than current faceted hat. Preserve cream seashell and blue rosette on viewer right, ivory repair stitches viewer left. BOTTOM RIGHT: keep original rose button round spectacles essentially unchanged. Precise 2x2 atlas, every part fully inside its quadrant with transparent padding at least 15px from all quadrant boundaries. All four parts same front-facing orientation. No whole character, no body, no neck, no hands, no background or drop shadow.

生成图集未严格遵守象限边界，因此运行器使用的是检查后的独立来源矩形，不依赖简单四等分。第二轮还在布局中扩大了发团、缩短帽子的投影高度，并保留歪头时的安全边距。

第三轮：保留有刘海版，并依据用户确认的无刘海整头预览，使用内置 imagegen 生成独立的无刘海前脸图层，保存为 `skins/original-no-bangs/face-no-bangs.png`。其他部件使用原图集。

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Create ONE replacement animation layer, using image 1's TOP RIGHT face component as the edit target. Image 2 is the user-approved final hairstyle reference (no bangs). Output ONLY the front face layer: the blank pale peach face, small ears, and the thick mauve curls framing BOTH sides and lower corners of the face. Remove ALL frontal bangs/fringe from the forehead, matching the clear forehead in reference 2. No center fringe points, no side-swept hair crossing the forehead. Keep the front face outline, ear position, side curl volume, mauve color and cel-shaded linework as close as possible to image 1's top right face layer. The face must be smooth and blank: NO glasses, NO button eyes, NO eyebrows, NO eyes, NO nose, NO mouth, NO blush. Those details are separate animated layers. Keep only the hair at the temples and sides, with a broad completely bare forehead and a softly rounded skin-colored upper head shape that can sit UNDER a hat. This is NOT the back-hair mass. Do NOT include any hat, shell, hair accessory, back-hair-only view, neck, body, shoulders, hands, labels, other components, or a component grid. Straight front view, symmetrical, the same rounded chibi head proportions as the top-right component of reference 1, with large thick side curls. Entire single component centered with at least 7% transparent padding on all sides. True transparent PNG background, no black or white fill, no checkerboard drawn. Preserve existing character design; only remove bangs.

第四轮：根据用户提供的书籍、盲杖和四种餐点参考，使用内置 imagegen 生成无手道具图集。最终文件为 `assets/props.png`，由运行器按 `props.json` 中的矩形取用。小手是程序绘制的独立米白色圆球，按用户反馈放大，并可隐藏与调整大小。早先的带手六部件草案没有放入最终程序。

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production animation PROP ATLAS for a chibi Helena Adams floating-head desktop pet. Reference 1 gives the existing approved chibi art style. Reference 2 shows her burgundy book and straight brass-handled brown cane. Match soft detailed 2D chibi art, warm dark outlines, gentle cel shading, muted cream, brass, burgundy, dusty-blue accents. ALL COMPONENTS MUST HAVE NO HANDS, NO ARMS, NO CUFFS, NO PEOPLE. Hands will be generated by the app as round mittens and may be hidden, so absolutely no hands attached to any object. No lettering, no labels, no cell lines, no scenery, no shadows/glow outside the objects, true transparent background. Row 1 left: open burgundy cloth book with thick ivory pages, seen slightly from above, open toward viewer; tiny embossed Braille dot groups on both pages instead of alphabet printing. ## 表情与朝向版补记

本轮没有调用图像生成或改写已有贴图。沿用两款发型和道具图集，在 `source/HeadMotion.cs` 中增加程序内三角网格变形、镜片内纽扣与缝线绘制、表情遮罩及泪滴；在 `App.cs` 中加入分级跟随、情绪与动作组合。预览由实际 WPF 渲染器输出。
